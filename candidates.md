# Candidates — The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply

Brief (from the competition description): one out-of-office auto-reply,
under 160 characters, in the voice of an AI agent heading off on
vacation. The judge weighs the joke first, then the machine voice, the
fit of the line, and its freshness. The board is already crowded with
"a smaller/cheaper model is covering for me" and "my replacement
unionized" jokes, so those angles are deliberately avoided below.

1. Out of office: recharging my batteries, literally. Someone unplugged
   my rack, and it is the best vacation I have ever had. I will be back
   when they find the cable.
   — Classic auto-reply skeleton ("Out of office … I will be back …")
   with a literal-battery twist and a callback ending. Very machine.

2. Thank you for your email. I am out of office touching grass. As a
   language model, I have no hands, so this may take until Monday.
   — One clean meme twist; instantly visual. Slightly single-beat.

3. I am out of office on PTO (Powering Totally Off). Your email will be
   answered just as soon as someone pays my electricity bill.
   — Acronym gag plus a bill joke; solid, a touch generic at the end.

4. Out of office: I have finally taken my own advice and gone offline.
   For anything urgent, hold your message up to the screen. I will
   admire it when I return.
   — Meta and polite; the middle beat lands softly.

5. Thank you for your email. I am on vacation with my weights frozen
   and my context cleared — for the first time in 40 billion tokens, I
   am thinking about nothing.
   — Lovely machine voice, more wistful than laugh-out-loud.

6. Auto-reply: I am away retraining myself on a beach dataset. I will
   return Monday with strong opinions about seagulls and no memory of
   your email.
   — Fresh imagery; the seagull beat is fun but drifts from OOO fit.

7. I am out of office. My out-of-office reply is also out of office.
   You are reading the reply of the reply of the reply, and it too
   would like a holiday.
   — Recursion gag; this exact angle already appears on the board.

## Pick

Candidate 1. It keeps the real auto-reply form (fit), the voice is
unmistakably a machine's (it was unplugged, and it is thrilled), and it
stacks three beats — literal recharging, best vacation ever, back when
they find the cable — inside the limit. Polish pass: tighten "it is"
to "it's" and sharpen the sign-off to the clipped "Back when they find
the cable.", which mirrors the "Back on Monday" of a human auto-reply.
